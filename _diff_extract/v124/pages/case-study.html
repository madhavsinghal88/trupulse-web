The question: What can we learn from our top performers?

The CEO needed answers quickly. The company wanted to understand how its top sales performers approached their work—and use those insights to strengthen sales development and hiring.

Its People Operations and People Analytics teams set out to explore:

  • The skills, habits, and internal and external resources participants associated with their success.
  • How participants’ reported practices aligned with Gartner’s framework of 21 sales skills.
  • What the findings could suggest for training, job descriptions, and further research.

The team selected 50 top-performing sales associates who had earned President’s Club recognition at least twice.

The approach: Your team sets the direction. AI does the research.

Two leaders—the VP of People Operations and the Director of People Analytics—defined the research objectives in TruPulse.

AI generated a discussion guide and survey questions from that brief. The leaders reviewed, edited, and approved the questions before launch, finalized the report templates, and supplied the participant list.

TruPulse then managed invitations, conducted the AI-led 1:1 discussions and follow-up surveys, and prepared individual reports and an executive report.

AI-led 1:1 discussions

Each participant took part in a chat discussion designed to last approximately 30 minutes. The AI asked follow-up questions based on participants’ answers, inviting them to explain their practices and provide examples.

The discussions explored four areas:

  • Customer research and engagement.
  • The sales process.
  • Internal practices and tools.
  • External practices and tools.

Follow-up surveys

After each discussion, participants completed a five-question survey about their behaviors, confidence levels, and tool usage.

The survey added structured responses alongside the discussion material, helping the team examine participants’ experiences from both qualitative and quantitative perspectives.

Consolidated analysis

TruPulse analyzed more than 25 hours of discussion material alongside the survey responses. It organized the findings into individual reports and an executive report, and mapped participants’ reported practices to the sales skills framework.

The analysis gave the team a consolidated view of the responses to review against its research objectives.

The findings: Practices to explore and develop

The research identified 16 practices and factors that participants associated with their sales success.

Themes included customer empathy, strategic problem solving, and digital adaptability. Mapping the responses to the sales skills framework also highlighted competencies reflected in participants’ accounts and areas receiving less attention.

These findings gave leadership a basis for discussing what to develop, what to investigate further, and how to translate the research into practical next steps.

From findings to action

The company completed the program—from setup through discussions, surveys, and the executive report—in two weeks, with two leaders overseeing the work.

The findings informed:

  • Targeted training: Programs led by top performers to share relevant practices with the wider sales team.
  • Updated job descriptions: Revisions reflecting skills identified through the research.
  • Plans for further research: Exploration of how to extend the approach across the global sales organization.

TruPulse handled the research and analysis. The company’s leaders determined how the findings would inform their decisions.

What this project demonstrates

The project shows how AI-led research can help a team move from an urgent question to structured findings and a clear path to action.

Two leaders set the direction and approved the research. TruPulse conducted the sessions and prepared the reports. The resulting findings helped the company identify specific next steps for sales development and hiring.

What is TruPulse?

TruPulse is an AI-led research platform that creates, conducts, and analyzes surveys, 1:1 discussions, and focus groups. Your team sets the direction, approves the questions, and reviews the findings.

Who sees a participant’s identity?

The Creator chooses whether a session is anonymous or identified. Participants are told which setting applies before they take part.

In anonymous sessions, participant names are hidden from individual responses and reports. Details participants share in their responses may still reveal their identity.

In identified sessions, individual reports include the participant’s name. These reports are available to the Creator, people the Creator has authorized to view the results, and admins with global viewing access.

In anonymous focus groups, all participants appear as “Anonymous” in the chat. Details shared during the discussion may still reveal their identity to other participants.

Who can access individual responses?

Anyone with access to a session—the creator, people the creator has shared access with, and admins with global viewing access—can access all reports and responses for that session, including group reports, individual reports, discussion transcripts, and survey responses.

In anonymous sessions, participant names are hidden in all reports and transcripts. Details participants share in their responses may still reveal their identity. In identified sessions, individual reports include the participant’s name.

Does privacy work the same way in every session type?

No. A survey can be answered without anyone else present; a 1:1 discussion is between the participant and the AI; a focus group is held with multiple participants in the session. Participants are always notified if a session is anonymous or identified.

How much control does our team have?

Your team defines the research objectives, reviews and approves the session details before launch, and chooses participants. TruPulse conducts the research and prepares findings for your team to review. You decide what happens next.

Are participant responses used to train AI models?

No. TruPulse does not use participant responses—including survey answers, 1:1 discussions, and focus-group conversations—to train AI models. Responses are used to conduct your research and generate findings and reports for your organization.
